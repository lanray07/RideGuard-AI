CODEX MASTER PROMPT
BUILD A PREMIUM iOS CYCLIST SAFETY APP — “RideGuard AI”

ROLE

Act as a senior iOS engineer, SwiftUI architect, mapping/GIS engineer,
cycling-safety product designer, privacy engineer, accessibility specialist,
StoreKit specialist and App Store creative director.

Build a production-quality premium iPhone and Apple Watch application called:

RIDEGUARD AI

Working tagline:

“Choose safer. Ride connected.”

CORE PRODUCT PROMISE

RideGuard AI helps urban cyclists:

• compare routes using safety-related factors
• understand why one route may be preferable to another
• see community-reported hazards and incidents
• report hazards quickly and safely
• share a live ride with trusted contacts
• automatically check in after rides
• receive alerts about relevant hazards
• build a personal picture of their cycling environment

This is NOT primarily:

• a fitness tracker
• a racing app
• a Strava competitor
• a generic maps app
• a social network

The central philosophy is:

“Don’t just show the fastest route.
Help me understand the safer options.”

The visual philosophy is:

“Real riders. Real streets. Clear safety information.”

==================================================
1. CRITICAL SAFETY PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Never claim that any route is “safe.”

No routing system can guarantee cyclist safety.

Use terminology such as:

• Lower Risk
• Higher Risk
• Safety Factors
• Risk Score
• Risk Indicators
• Lower-risk option
• Fewer reported hazards

Avoid:

“Safe Route”
“Guaranteed Safe”
“Accident-Free”
“Safest Possible Route”

Every score must be explainable.

Never manufacture accident statistics, crime statistics, collision data,
road conditions or community reports.
