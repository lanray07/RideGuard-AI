# RideGuard AI privacy policy

Effective 10 September 2026. This policy describes the current local-mode RideGuard AI version maintained by O. Bankole.

RideGuard stores ride history, road observations, optional report photos, saved places, trusted contact details and preferences on your device. It does not operate an account, analytics service, advertising service, cloud report feed, remote AI processor or live-location sharing service in this version.

## Location and maps

When you request your location for planning, RideGuard asks for location permission. A ride you explicitly start can continue location updates while the screen is locked. Ending the ride stops these updates. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings.

Destination searches and route requests use Apple MapKit. Apple processes those requests under its own terms and privacy policy. The application does not send your ride history or contacts to a RideGuard server. Demo maps, routes and reports are clearly labelled and are not observations of current conditions.

## Photos and voice

You choose a report photo through the system photo picker. The app stores a bounded copy without EXIF metadata on your device. RideGuard synthesises short spoken responses and does not retain raw audio or transcripts. Siri is a separate Apple service governed by your device settings and Apple's policies.

## Sharing you choose

If you use the system share sheet, the selected app and recipient receive the content you choose. Sharing a current location includes a precise location; you choose the recipient. No trusted contact receives automatic location updates or overdue messages in this version. RideGuard cannot recall exported or shared copies and cannot verify their delivery.

## Retention, export and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them. You can delete individual rides or reports, export local records after device authentication, or use You → Delete all local data. Deleting all data also ends an active ride. System backups may retain copies according to your device's backup settings. Copies sent to others are outside these controls.

## Purchases

StoreKit may query available products and verified entitlements through Apple. This preview does not offer unfinished cloud features for sale. Apple manages payment information; RideGuard does not receive card details.

## Contact and changes

For product support or privacy questions, use the [RideGuard support tracker](https://github.com/lanray07/RideGuard-AI/issues). Do not post precise locations, phone numbers, private ride history or other sensitive data in a public issue. If new cloud features are introduced, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures must be updated before those features are enabled.
